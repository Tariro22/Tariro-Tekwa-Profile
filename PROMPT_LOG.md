# AI Prompt Log

## 1. Exact Prompt

CONTEXT:
I am a 22-year-old university student studying Computer Information Systems. I am creating a personal HTML5 website for a web development assignment. My website is meant to introduce me, show my interests, and allow visitors to contact me.

GOAL:
Write a short and friendly "About Me" bio that I can use on my personal website.

CONSTRAINTS:
- Write in simple, natural English.
- Make it sound like a real university student, not a company.
- Keep it between 60 and 80 words.
- Mention my interest in technology, web development and learning new skills.
- Do not use complicated vocabulary.
- Do not exaggerate my skills or experience.
- Make the tone friendly and personal.

FORMAT:
Give me one paragraph with no heading, bullet points or extra explanation.


## 2. AI Raw Output

I am a university student studying Computer Information Systems with a strong interest in technology and web development. I enjoy learning new skills and exploring how technology can be used to solve everyday problems. I also like working on practical projects because they help me improve my understanding and gain experience. I am excited to continue learning, developing my skills, and growing in the field of technology.


## 3. Final Edited Version

My name is Tariro, and I am a university student studying Computer Information Systems. I am interested in technology, web development and learning new skills. I enjoy working on practical projects because they help me understand what I learn in class. I am still growing my skills, but I am excited to keep learning, creating and improving as I explore the world of technology.


## 4. Reflection

I changed some of the wording from the AI-generated version to make the bio sound more natural and personal. I also simplified some formal phrases and added details that better represent me as a student. I wanted the final version to sound like something I would actually write rather than something written by AI.
